1. Who I Am

I (Scamguard) operate as a crowdsourced scam intelligence platform, allowing users to submit and search scam reports. As the sole operative, I am responsible for handling your personal data in accordance with this policy and applicable UK data protection laws.

2. Lawful Bases For Processing

I process your personal data based on the following lawful bases under UK GDPR:

  • Account and authentication: Upon registration, users submit their email address (used for verification) and create a password to access their account. No further data is collected with regards to IP addresses, Full Name, or any protected characteristics.
  • Scam report submission and moderation: Users are able to submit reports when logged in, which can be submitted anonymously. These reports are used to identify and track scam activity, improve platform functionality, and assist other users in staying up to date.
  • Security, abuse prevention, fraud detection: ScamGuard uses email verification to ensure the legitimacy of user accounts and to prevent fraudulent activity.
  • Analytics/product improvement: I do not store analytics data that can identify individual users, nor do I currently track user behaviour on the site.

3. Data I Collect

  • Identity data: Username, email, password
  • User-submitted content: Scam report titles, descriptions, user-submitted websites, user-submitted usernames/scammer names.
  • Technical data: Logs are maintained which may include IP addresses, browser information, and other technical details necessary for security and troubleshooting.
  • Admin and audit data: Standard infrastructure/service logs may still process technical metadata (e.g., IP, user-agent, timestamps)

4. How I Use Your Data

[Explain each processing activity clearly and in plain English.]

  • Operate accounts and provide platform features.
  • Publish/search scam intelligence records.
  • Run AI-assisted analysis workflows to classify risk.
  • Detect misuse, protect system integrity, and enforce terms.
  • Respond to legal requests and regulatory obligations.

5. Data Sharing And Processors

  • Hosting provider: Github Pages
  • Database/auth provider: Supabase
  • AI provider(s): Local Ollama AI, running Qwen2.5vl:7b
  • Backend provider: Render

Under no circumstance will data ever be sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. Where required under legal guidance, I may share data with law enforcement or regulatory authorities to assist with investigations into fraudulent activity.

6. International Transfers

Data shall only leave the UK to be processed by the server providers, based offshores in Germany. These are servers maintained in compliance with EU data protection standards and accessed solely by myself.

7. Retention Periods

  • Account records: Account records are kept until deleted by the user to maintain a comprehensive archive, however upon either user request or inactivity for 6 months, the account shall be closed.
  • Scam report content: Scam report content shall be maintained indefinitely, however should an account be deleted or closed, the reports will be anonymised.
  • Evidence uploads: In order to provide a comprehensive archive, user uploads are saved indefinitely unless requested by the user with valid security concerns.
  • Security and audit logs: Logs are maintained for 14 days under provider constraints.

8. Data Security

Sensitive user data is encrypted such as passwords used for login, and through "security by design" practices, unnecessary data is not collected. Access to data is strictly limited to myself as the sole operator, and all processing is done in compliance with industry best practices for security. In the event of a data breach, I will promptly notify affected users and relevant authorities as required by law.

9. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

You may have rights to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request erasure in certain cases.
  • Restrict or object to processing in certain cases.
  • Data portability where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

Users are able to contact me at scamguardweb@gmail.com to request, correct, restrict, erase or delete data. Data is only collected upon user submission upon creating an account or submitting a report, and as such consent is assumed upon submission of data.

10. Cookies And Tracking

Cookies are only used to identify and preserve user sessions. No further cookies are used, such as for tracking or marketing purposes.
